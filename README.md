🎮 Game Features

Core Mechanics:

Devolve 4 traits: Strength, Speed, Intelligence, and Vision
5 unique levels with different survival challenges
Strategic weakness: Each level requires you to weaken specific traits to survive
Dynamic creature transformation: Your appearance changes as you devolve
Reverse boss fights: Challenges are "won" by becoming useless to threats

Level Progression:

The Hunting Grounds - Predators ignore weak creatures (devolve Strength)
The Maze of Minds - Smart creatures trigger traps (devolve Intelligence)
The Toxic Garden - Large bodies absorb more poison (devolve Strength)
The Blinding Storm - Clear vision causes madness (devolve Vision)
The Final Test - Must re-evolve exactly ONE trait above 50 for the twist ending!

Visual Polish:

Grotesque-cute creature transformations (🦎 → 🐁 → 🐛)
Atmospheric environments with floating threats
Pulsing challenge notifications
Trait bars that change color (red = dangerous, green = safely weak)

Controls:

Arrow keys to move your creature
Click "Devolve" buttons to strategically lose traits
Navigate through each environment to trigger challenges
